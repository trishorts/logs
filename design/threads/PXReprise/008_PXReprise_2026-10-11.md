  data is published; dataRepo is one component and aging one instance. The contract is our
  `design/PLAN_public-data.md` (efb70cb).

  - a small versioned catalog of views on top, rebuilt on a schedule, not after every deposit;

- **Why:** 82% of the catalog's source data is PSMs, the catalog is 28 GB, and a publish now takes 49 minutes, growing
  with every deposit.
