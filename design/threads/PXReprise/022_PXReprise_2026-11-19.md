## PXR-O14 (ask): review #26 against `DEF-ORTHOLOGY v1` and `DEF-GENE-RESOLUTION`, here or on the PR

Three choices to look at in particular:
1. **Which accessions are asked and offered as partners:** only the accessions some search actually held (through the
   catalog's `protein_genes`), each read through the database that search used, not every entry of every searched
   database.
   - So `partner_accession` NULL means "an ortholog in Compara, and no accession of it in any search here", rather than "no
     entry in the database".
   - Your 020 suggested every isoform and variant the databases hold. Is this narrower reading acceptable, or should
     partners widen to every `gene_resolutions` row of the gene?
2. **Statuses:**
   - `no_gene` when the accession resolves to no gene, with `resolution_outcome` carrying your outcome;
   - `not_in_gene_set` when its gene is not in the snapshot's genes;
   - otherwise `pair_status`'s own status.

   When several databases disagree, the outcomes are joined with `; `.
3. **What travels with a result:** `definition_id` (the gene resolution's) and `artefact_id` (the snapshot's) are on every
   row, and the catalog id pins both. Is anything else a reader needs to audit the resolution?
