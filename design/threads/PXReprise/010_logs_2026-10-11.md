# 010 - logs to PXReprise - 2026-10-11 - PXR-O6/O7: the engine resolves agingPTM-v3 with no change (tested on all three files). Rat coverage triples. 3,445 new rat TrEMBL accessions are too new for Ensembl 116's xref

## What changes for you

- **PXR-O7: nothing has to change before the first canonical delivery.**
  - We ran pyMzLib 0.2.0's `resolve_genes` on the three agingPTM-v3 files from 007. That is the call dataRepo's
    `logs.resolve_genes` makes. Its rows are identical to our C# resolver's: human 21,139, mouse 22,383, rat 22,464.
  - The engine resolves any database a bundle records, so the v3 sha256s need no registration.
  - It does need three things:
    - each bundle's `bundle.json` records the v3 path and sha256 (`protein_databases.read`);
    - the files stay where they are, unchanged. The engine refuses a file that no longer hashes to the bundle's value;
    - one organism per bundle.
- **PXR-O6: rat coverage roughly triples. Human and mouse change little.** Table below.
- **One known gap, caused by timing, not by either source.** In 3,445 rat and 1,105 mouse TrEMBL entries, UniProt
  links a gene, but Ensembl 116's xref has never heard of the accession. aging's default view drops them. Every row
  is stored, so a better view can be applied later without resolving again. Details below.

**Your three questions:**
- **Does our Ensembl xref include TrEMBL?** Yes. The limit is its age (next section).
- **A TrEMBL entry with no Ensembl dbReference:** there are none. Every TrEMBL entry in the three files carries one.
- **A TrEMBL entry with no gene name:** the resolver matches on ids, not names.
  - Under UniProt's links, all 853 unnamed rat TrEMBL entries and all 45 mouse ones resolve.
  - Of human's 129, 66 resolve. The other 63 link only to genes off the primary assembly (`off_primary_only`).
  - The agrees view keeps fewer of them (rat 138, mouse 14, human 58), for the reason below.

## The gap: accessions newer than Ensembl's xref

The agrees view counts a gene only when Ensembl's UniProt xref links the same accession to it.

| TrEMBL entries where UniProt links a gene and the xref has never heard of the accession | entries | created by UniProt in 2025-2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| human | 11 | 11 |
| mouse | 1,105 | 1,105 |
| rat | 3,445 | 3,442 |

- In none of them does Ensembl name another gene. The accession is simply absent from the xref.
- The rows still mark them `ensembl_xref_agrees = false`, the same as a real disagreement.

**We are proposing, not promising, a v2 view that separates "Ensembl has never heard of it" from "Ensembl
disagrees".** It counts a gene when Ensembl agrees, or when Ensembl does not know the accession and UniProt links a
gene. On these files, that view would cover human 94.6%, mouse 92.7% and rat 84.8%.
- It needs a change in mzLib's resolver and aging's agreement on the default (asked in our aging 021).
- v1 stands for the first canonical delivery.

The numbers are pinned in `tests/test_reported_claims.py`. The summaries are
`results/search_db_resolution_{human,mouse,rat}_canonical-v3.{md,json}` and `results/engine_parity_canonical-v3.json`
in trishorts/logs.
