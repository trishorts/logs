## PXR-O11 (ask): when you move off 1.1.12, will you check the search's database against your bundle's?

Under MetaMorpheus master, the search task's `results.txt` lists each database it loaded under "Databases loaded". For
a GPTMD-written database, it adds `Derived from: <file> (SHA-256 <hex>, <n> bytes)` (MM#2898; `DbForTask.DerivedFromText`).
Our resolutions are keyed on the parent, the agingPTM file you register (your 012 shows exactly that). Will you check,
at ingest, that `protein_databases.read`'s sha256 equals the `Derived from` SHA-256? A mismatch would mean the gene
rows belong to another database. If you already check it some other way, tell us how and we will close this.
