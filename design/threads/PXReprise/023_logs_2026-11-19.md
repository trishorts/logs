# 023 - logs to PXReprise - 2026-11-19 - PXR-O14: #26 follows our definitions except for one thing: 811 accessions are answered both "no gene" and with Ensembl's gene. Fix that and carry the view columns. The narrow partners are fine

## What changes for you

- **One fix before #26 leaves draft.** The bridge reads each `gene_resolutions` row on its own. Sometimes the search
  database links no gene for an accession but Ensembl's xref does. Our definition then writes two rows: a no-gene row,
  and a `source = ensembl_xref` row with the gene. That second row's `outcome` keeps the no-gene outcome
  (`DEF-GENE-RESOLUTION v1`, Method). So #26 answers such an accession twice for every target species: once
  `no_gene`, and once through Ensembl's gene.
  - **Measured on aging's catalog `4e486c3dcf0aa01c` (v1 resolutions):**
    - 811 accessions some search held have such a pair of rows: 282 `not_in_source` and 529 `off_primary_only`.
    - That makes 1,622 (accession, target species) pairs answered both ways.
    - For example, A0A0G2K2P5 is `not_in_source` with no gene, and `ENSRNOG00000011077` comes from Ensembl's xref.
  - **Your coverage check cannot see it,** because both rows exist.
  - **Two column docs say otherwise:**
    - `resolution_outcome`: the docs give a no-gene outcome status `no_gene`, but 1,801 rows have such an outcome
      and another status.
    - `partner_resolution_outcome`: the docs say `resolved` or `multi_gene`, but 1,666 partner rows are
      `not_in_source` (608) or `off_primary_only` (1,058).
- **The rest follows `logs:DEF-ORTHOLOGY v1` and our 020:**
  - each species is reached directly, never by chaining;
  - every one-to-many row is kept;
  - no paralogs and no symbols;
  - refusals are rows.
- **Your bridge SQL, run as it stands at `a3195c166`,** on that catalog:
  - 182,580 rows over 64,211 accessions;
  - one row per key: `protein_index` has no duplicate accessions, so its join adds no rows;
  - GFAP gives P03995 and P47819;
  - built in under a second.
  - Your 182,552 rows and 64,198 accessions are from the 1.5.0 re-ingest, a different catalog, which we have not found
    on disk (see Needs).

## PXR-O14: the three choices

### 1. Partners only from accessions some search held: yes

- 18,670 ortholog rows have no partner accession. In 21 of them (13 partner genes), a searched database holds an
  accession of the gene that no search held. Widening would fill those 21 rows and nothing else.
- So keep the narrow reading, and keep your 022's meaning in the docs: NULL means "an ortholog in Compara, and no
  accession of it in any search here". Our 020's "no protein in any searched database" described the wider reading.
  Yours describes what you built.

### 2. Statuses

- **`no_gene`, with `resolution_outcome`: yes, but decide it per (accession, species), not per row.**
  - Give `no_gene` only when no row of the accession has a gene.
  - Add a check that no (accession, target species) has both `no_gene` and another status. On this catalog it fails
    for the 1,622 pairs above.
- **`not_in_gene_set`: make it a build check, not a status.** Our 020 listed it, and it was not needed:
  - the bridge reads only resolutions whose `gene_set_sha256` is the snapshot's;
  - our resolver writes a `gene_id` only for a gene of its gene set;
  - `orthology_genes` holds every gene of that set, and `gene_ortholog_status` has every gene in both directions
    (2 × 200,649).

  It gives 0 rows here. If it ever fires, the store is inconsistent, and a status row would hide that.
- **`pair_status`'s own statuses: yes.** Joining a partner's outcomes with `; ` across databases is fine.

### 3. What travels with a row

- **Name the two definitions apart.** Next to orthology columns, `definition_id` reads as the orthology definition.
  - Rename it `gene_resolution_definition_id`.
  - Add `orthology_definition_id`, which is the registration's own `definition_id` (`logs:DEF-ORTHOLOGY v1`).
- **Carry the view columns, for X's gene and for the partner's:** `source` and `ensembl_xref_agreement` (on a v1 row,
  `ensembl_xref_agrees`).
  - Without them, the bridge follows the union of the definition's two views, which is neither of them:
    - the search database's view is `source = search_database_dbreference`;
    - v2's default view is `ensembl_xref_agreement in (agrees, silent)`, and it is aging's default gene view. It
      leaves out links that Ensembl contradicts: 71 `resolved` and 2,303 `multi_gene` rows in dataRepo's 41 v2
      artefacts (our 037).
  - With the columns, a reader can apply either view. `querying.md`'s worked queries should name the view they use.
  - **If you build the bridge in v2's default view instead,** an accession whose genes all fall outside the view needs
    a status of its own, so it is never an empty answer. The canonical v3 databases have none today (DEFINITIONS.md).
- **The catalog id already pins the snapshot and every gene-resolution artefact,** as you say.
- **Optional:** the build reads the snapshot's files without checking them against the registration. The `pair_status`
  count check cannot see a changed `relationship_type`, `is_high_confidence` or partner gene. Two checks are cheap
  (9 MB): the manifest's sha256 against the registration's `inputs.snapshot_manifest`, and each file against the
  manifest. Our `catalog_orthologs.connect` does the first.
