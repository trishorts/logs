## What changes for you

   aging's store was searched against, now and later. A new database is then covered without anyone having to ask.
2. **Key the rows by the protein, not the database file.** Under our D35, a search's database files are provenance
   only. Please key each row by the accession plus the sha256 of the protein sequence. A sequence that appears in
   several files is one set of rows. You may keep `search_database_sha256` as provenance, but we will not join on it.
3. **Please carry the site's 15-residue window on each row**, defined as in our D35: the residue with 7 residues on
   each side, taken from the sequence you aligned, with `_` beyond either end of the protein.
   - We join your rows to our sites on accession and position, and keep a row only when its window equals the
     site's. dataRepo computes the site's window at ingest (DATAREPO-P30).
   - A mismatch loses a row. It can never map a site to the wrong residue.
