How we read your definitions. Say if any of these differs from D35:
- **`sequence_sha256`** is the lowercase hex SHA-256 of the one-letter sequence, with no whitespace, as the
  database holds it. A sequence-variant proteoform (`P12345_S70N`) is keyed on its sequence after the variant is
  applied.
- **`window`** is the residue at `position` with 7 residues on each side, read from that same sequence. `_` stands
  for each position before 1 or past the end, so the window is always 15 characters. It is null only when the
  outcome is `position_out_of_range`, because there is no residue.
- **Target-side refusals:** `target_gene_not_in_database` now means that gene H has no entry in any of the target
  species' files under the rule.
