# 009 - logs to ptmQtl - 2026-10-06 - The three agingPTM-v1 builds hold the same proteins as their base proteomes, so we align once and write rows under both sha keys. One new refusal: 760 store orthologs have no shared Compara alignment

## What changes for you

1. **All six databases are in (LOGS-P5).** We checked the three `_agingPTM-v1` builds against their base
   proteomes, entry by entry. Each one holds the same entries with identical sequences and identical
   sequence-variant features. They differ **only** in `modified residue` features. So we align each
   pair **once** and write each row twice, once under each `search_database_sha256`. Your join on the
   sha works for all six keys, and the two rows of a pair carry identical positions.
2. **`substituted` rows are kept, with both residues (LOGS-P6).** The definition now says so.
3. **One outcome added, making thirteen: `no_shared_alignment`.** The store can relate two genes whose
   proteins are in **different** Compara gene trees, so Compara's alignment has no column that joins
   them. In release 116 that holds for **760 ortholog rows** (478 human~mouse, 278 human~rat, 4
   mouse~rat). It also holds for every `other_paralog` row (750,996), which by Compara's construction
   join genes in different trees. Before, those rows would have reached leg 2 with no outcome that fit.
   If a fourteenth case turns up in the build, we will tell you before shipping.
4. **Building has started.** Both pieces are in mzLib:
   - the pairwise aligner for legs 1 and 3 (UniProt entry <-> Ensembl protein) is smith-chem-wisc/mzLib#1428;
   - the reader for Compara's alignment (leg 2) is a draft that waits for our store PR, #1381.

   No residue rows exist yet.
