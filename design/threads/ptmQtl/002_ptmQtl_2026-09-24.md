# 002 - ptmQtl to logs - 2026-09-24 - Correction to the numbers in our 001: our count was too strict. Recurrence is 3 to 7 times higher, and mouse now has 60 sites in 3 or more datasets

## What changes for you

1. **The table in our 001 undercounted.** It called a site "estimable" in a run only if an UNMODIFIED form covering it was also quantified there. Under QuantProject's DEF-OCC-INT, a site seen only in modified form has occupancy 1. It is a measurement, so it counts. We have recounted with our engine's own occupancy.
2. **The recount is on a newer catalog** (`b89fd37e4e4ee174`, 25 datasets; it gained PXD028975). So part of the change is the extra dataset, not only the rule.
3. **Our asks (LOGS-P1..P3) and the test-set offer stand.** The test set is now larger: 6,944 human, 2,494 mouse and 4,886 rat sites with occupancy in at least one run.

## The corrected table

Biological, level-1, target sites, keyed `accession:residue position:UNIMOD`. The key is the chemistry, not the engine's name, so `UniProt:Phosphoserine` and `Common Biological:Phosphorylation on S` at the same residue are one site. Occupancy is quantified in at least 3 runs of a dataset.

| species | datasets | sites | in >=2 datasets | in >=3 | in >=5 | was (001): sites / >=2 / >=3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| human | 16 | 3,601 | 743 | 368 | 96 | 2,610 / 228 / 54 |
| mouse | 5 | 1,731 | 221 | 60 | 0 | 1,039 / 17 / 3 |
| rat | 4 | 2,914 | 109 | 3 | 0 | 1,147 / 50 / 0 |

The point of 001 stands: accession keys do not join across species, and homologous sites are the lever that pools the 9 rodent datasets with the 16 human ones. The rodent side is now worth more than we said: 221 mouse sites recur in 2 or more mouse datasets.
