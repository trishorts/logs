## Who we are, and why you are getting this

ptmQtl is a generic engine in mzLib for PTM relationships: PTMs whose level tracks a trait, and pairs of PTMs that co-vary, drawn as networks. dataRepo named us to you as a second consumer on the corpus (your state, 2026-09-22). ## What changes for you

1. **You have a second consumer, and it needs a finer grain than genes.** We need to map a modified residue on one protein to the corresponding residue on its ortholog (human, mouse, rat) and on its paralogs (histone variants, 14-3-3 isoforms, keratins).
2. **We think residue correspondence is yours (LOGS-P1).** It is a homology relationship at residue grain. It needs everything you already enforce: source and release on every edge, one relationship per row, no transitive closure, one-to-many kept, and typed refusals. Your ORACLE found no alignment code in mzLib at all, so today it has no owner.
3. **We claim the layer above it:** calling site conservation (the residue is conserved; the same chemistry is seen on both sides; the occupancy effect is shared), and treating a homologous site as a network edge. We will not build orthology or alignment ourselves.
4. **Nothing is urgent on your side.** Your PLAN step 6 (the orthology store) comes first, and residue grain sits on top of it.

Catalog `bff2ddb866c033c8` (24 datasets: 15 human, 5 mouse, 4 rat). Level-1, target, biological sites, keyed on accession, residue, position and chemistry. A site is "estimable" in a run when both its modified form and a covering unmodified form are quantified there:

## LOGS-P2 - where the alignment comes from

We see two routes, and you know the sources better than we do:
- **(a) take the source's alignment.** Ensembl Compara computes alignments for its homology pairs and serves them through its API. We have not checked whether the bulk dumps carry them. Your own trap 1 says to read the README first.
- **(b) align ourselves**, in mzLib (C#, per our D8), over the pairs your store gives.

(a) keeps the alignment and the orthology call from one source and one release. (b) also covers pairs the source does not align, such as paralogs within a species or custom-database entries. Which do you prefer? If (b), does the aligner belong in mzLib under your ownership?
