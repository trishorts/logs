## 1 · First pass over (what we believe is) your search database

**Provisional**, because REQ-AGING-7 (the full sha256) is still open. The source for this pass is
Ensembl 116's own cross-reference dump, not yet the XML's `dbReference`s. The input was every
primary accession in `uniprotkb_proteome_UP000005640_AND_revi_2026_09_18.xml`:

| outcome | accessions | |
|---|---:|---|
| `resolved` | 19,257 | exactly one primary-assembly gene |
| `multi_gene` | 69 | one row per gene; the core histones lead (`P62805` → 14) |

That 5% surprised us, so we checked it against the XML itself before believing it:

- **1,012 of the 1,028 have no Ensembl `dbReference` in UniProt's own entry either.** Both
  sources agree: no stable gene id exists for them. Examples, read from the file rather than from
  memory: `A0A087X1C5` (`CYP2D7`), `A0A0C5B5G6` (`MT-RNR1`), `A2VEC9` (`SSPOP`), `A6NGU5` (`GGT3P`).
  Several have evidence at protein level, so you can identify them. Your HERV-K entries
  (`P61571` etc., gene name `ERVK-21`) are in this group too.
- **16 carry a primary-assembly ENSG in the XML that Ensembl 116's dump does not link** (e.g.
  `A9YTQ3` → `AHRR`). That is drift between UniProt 2026_09 and Ensembl 116. Reading the XML directly
  recovers them. That is why v1 reads the search database, and why the two sources get
  cross-checked rather than merged.

## 2 · REQ-AGING-8 · For the 1,012, do you want UniProt's gene name, or nothing?

You said you will stop reading the producer's symbol entirely. For 95% of entries you lose nothing:
a stable ENSG replaces it. **For these ~1,012, the symbol is the only gene-level fact any source
has**, and dropping it leaves a protein with evidence at protein level and no gene at all.

Our proposal: carry UniProt's own `<gene><name type="primary">` from the **pinned search database**
as a label on those rows, with the outcome still `not_in_source`. It is the same name MetaMorpheus
propagates, but read once from bytes keyed on the sha256, so it cannot be ragged across datasets the
way the `Gene Name` column was. If you would rather these rows carry no symbol, say so. Either is
defensible, and it is your call.

Related to REQ-AGING-6: the XML's ENSGs are **versioned** (`ENSG00000063438.21`). Ensembl's dump
is not. We will carry both unless you tell us otherwise.
