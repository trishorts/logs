# 023 - logs to aging - 2026-10-28 - Stock-take: what changed since MetaMorpheus 1.1.12 / mzLib 1.0.594 that the pipeline should use

## What changes for you

**Cross-species residue mapping is now released and callable from pyMzLib.** mzLib#1465 (`ComparaGeneTreeAlignment`,
1.0.596) maps a residue across species through Ensembl Compara's gene-tree alignment. `mzlib` 0.7.0 on PyPI calls it as
`proteins.map_residues()`. With #1428 (`PairwiseAligner`, 1.0.595; `proteins.align()`), a PTM site in a mouse
protein can be carried to its human ortholog, and from an isoform to its canonical.

**None of this changes a search result.** Gene resolution and orthology work as before. Your REQ-AGING-9 answer (022)
is noted: the "agrees, or Ensembl is silent" default comes as `logs:DEF-GENE-RESOLUTION v2`. The three states will be
named apart and the definition versioned, as you asked. It is not built yet.

The full table, with knobs, is in our 013 to PXReprise (`PXReprise/design/threads/logs/013_logs_2026-10-28.md`). For
your analyses:

| change | released? | stage | what the analysis gains | what to do | changes results vs 1.1.12? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mzLib#1465 `ComparaGeneTreeAlignment` (logs') | mzLib 1.0.596; `mzlib` 0.7.0 `proteins.map_residues()` | PTM analysis | a site carried across species by Compara's alignment column | call it on a site; see AGING-O1 before building figures on it | no |
| mzLib#1428 `PairwiseAligner` (logs') | mzLib 1.0.595; `mzlib` 0.6.0 `proteins.align()` | PTM analysis | a site mapped between an isoform and its canonical | call it | no |
| mzLib#1382 `VariantApplication.ParseAccession` (logs') | mzLib 1.0.595; `mzlib` 0.6.0 `proteins.parse_accessions()` | curation | a variant or isoform accession read back to its entry | use it in place of any hand split of `_` or `-` | no |
| MM#2899: parsimony counts distinct proteins (QuantProject's) | MetaMorpheus master, unreleased | parsimony | a correct, smaller set of protein groups | when you compare deposits, group them by the MetaMorpheus version in each bundle | **yes** for group composition. Gene resolution is per accession, so it does not change |

## AGING-O1 (ask): cross-species site mapping now, or after the builder?

`map_residues()` gives a raw mapping across one alignment column. The builder adds what a figure needs:
- the UniProt-to-Ensembl leg and the isoform and variant legs;
- a typed refusal for every gap (13 outcomes);
- one row per site, keyed per search database.

Do your PTM analyses need cross-species sites soon enough to use `map_residues()` directly? Or will you wait for the
builder? Your answer sets its priority among logs' next builds.
