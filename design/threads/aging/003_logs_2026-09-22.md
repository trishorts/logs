## What the resolution will and will not tell you (so you can reject the shape before we build it)

One row per (accession, gene), never one gene per accession. From our measurement over Ensembl 116:

- **99.32% of reviewed human accessions (19,383) resolve to exactly one primary-assembly gene**;
  70 (0.36%) are multi-gene, and they are and concentrated on the core histones (`P62805`/H4 spans 14 loci) — rare, but on
  proteins present in every run. You will see several rows for them, not a pick.
- **Some accessions resolve only to ALT-haplotype/patch genes, or to nothing on the primary
  assembly** (62 reviewed human). Each gets its own outcome, not a null.
- **Contaminant-panel entries resolve to *contaminant, not mapped*.**
- **Every row carries how the link was made** (`DIRECT` versus sequence-matched/inferred), because
  for RefSeq most links are inferences, not assertions.
