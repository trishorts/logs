## 2 · The one thing you must know: the source changes the multi-gene count

005 resolved your entries against **Ensembl's own cross-reference table**. The table you will get reads
the gene links **your search XML itself carries**. The two sources agree on almost everything:

- 1,012 entries with no gene id in either source;
- 62 found only on alternate haplotypes;
- the same 69 genuine multi-gene entries.

They differ in one place. **UniProt links an accession to every Ensembl transcript that encodes it.**
That includes readthrough genes (`MSH5` is also linked to `MSH5-SAPCD1`, `TNFSF12` to
`TNFSF12-TNFSF13`), unnamed novel genes and identical paralogs. Ensembl's table assigns the accession
to one gene. So:

| outcome | 005 (Ensembl's table) | your table (the search XML's links) |
|---|---:|---:|
| `resolved` | 19,257 | 18,988 |
| `multi_gene` | **69** | **350** |
| `off_primary_only` | 62 | 62 |
| `not_in_source` | 1,028 | 1,016 |

Neither answer is wrong, and neither is dropped. Every gene row carries **`ensembl_xref_agrees`**:

- Of the 350, **281 have exactly one gene that Ensembl's table agrees with.** Those are the extra
  links.
- **69** are genuinely multi-gene. They are the same 69 as in 005, core histones first.
- Keep only rows where `ensembl_xref_agrees = true` and you get 005's answer back for 20,412 of
  20,416 entries. The other 4 are entries whose XML carries no Ensembl link at all.

Following your own rule, this is a column and not a mode. Whether a readthrough gene counts is your
filter to apply.

## 3 · What the other columns mean

- `ensembl_xref_agrees` is **empty, not `false`**, on rows with no gene, and whenever the table was
  built without Ensembl's cross-references. "We didn't check" and "it disagrees" stay distinct.
- `ensembl_xref_info_type` shows how Ensembl made the link: `DIRECT` is an assertion,
  `SEQUENCE_MATCH` is an inference.
- **Sequence variants** are included. `LoadProteinXML` expands your 20,416 entries into 52,359
  proteoforms (e.g. `A0A087X1C5_S70N`). Each one resolves through its entry and keeps its own
  verbatim accession.
