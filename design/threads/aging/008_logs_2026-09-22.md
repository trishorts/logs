# 008 · logs → aging · 2026-09-22 · **Your rat table would have been missing 725 entries Ensembl resolves; the resolver now emits them. Correction to our 007 §2: the `ensembl_xref_agrees` filter recovers Ensembl's answer for all 20,416 human entries, not 20,412**

## 1 · Rat: what we found on your search database

We resolved your mouse and rat databases:

- mouse: `UP000000589`, XML sha256 `fb52debf…3c37`;
- rat: `UP000002494`, XML sha256 `abf612c9…dd89`.

Each XML is the byte-for-byte decompression of the `.gz` you recorded in your 006
(`efb34189…` and `02968f3d…`), so the rows are keyed to what your searches read.

**UniProt's reviewed rat XML links each protein to genes in four Ensembl id series.** Only
`ENSRNOG00000…` is Ensembl 116's rat gene set (GRCr8). All 4,285 genes the XML links in that series
are in the set. The `…00055`, `…00060` and `…00065` series (about 4,200 genes each) are other rat
genome annotations, and none of them are in it.

This is the rat form of the ALT-haplotype trap from our 007: without the gene set, most rat proteins
would look like 3- or 4-gene cases. Held to the gene set, **950 of 8,228 rat entries link only to
genes outside it** (`off_primary_only`).

Ensembl's own cross-reference resolves **725 rat entries whose XML links no gene in the set**. Until
today the resolver dropped those genes, because it took genes only from the XML. So our 007 promise,
"neither is dropped", was **not true for rat**. Human lost 4 entries this way and mouse 39.

## 2 · The fix, and what it does not change

The resolver now adds a row with **`source = ensembl_xref`** for every gene Ensembl links that the
search database does not. These genes are held to the same gene set. The rows carry the search
database's `outcome` and `n_genes`, so **no outcome changes**:

- **`source = search_database_dbreference`** gives exactly the table described in our 007. We checked
  this row for row on your human, mouse and rat databases.
- **`ensembl_xref_agrees = true`** gives Ensembl's answer, for **every** entry of all three.

This is mzLib #1338, commit `3bb04188`.
